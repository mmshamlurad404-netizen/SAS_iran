Executive read

This edition combines 200 audited services with 110 legacy services re-underwritten against Latka Iran benchmarks, fully loaded 1405 payroll (Tamin, Eidi, Sanavat), channel commissions, SAYAD cheque risk and 7-9% monthly inflation. Average standalone EBITDA margin is 24.6%; shared-engine portfolio EBITDA reaches 66.7B Toman per month.

Tier distribution is 112 P1, 155 P2 and 43 P3. By verdict, 157 services are strong standalone wedges, 127 are viable standalone or strong portfolio add-ons, and 26 only work as shared-engine add-ons. Ten services post negative standalone EBITDA (two audited and eight re-underwritten) and must be managed through portfolio sales; thin-margin tools follow the same route.

English USD columns use the project rate of 270,000 Toman; the source audit used 203,100. Toman remains the authoritative currency throughout this project.

Methodology

Models use a 12-month ramp with Latka-calibrated customer counts (22-40 enterprise, 115-205 SME, 340-480 prosumer/micro), blended ARPU and 4-11% monthly churn. Costs include domestic GPU with a 25% buffer, NIN, SMS/Shaparak, 12-18% partner commissions, fully loaded tech/sales/support payroll, Tehran office and marketing. Engines 1-4 and 6 stay local; engines 5A/5B are hybrid except in tax, Moadian, healthcare, legal and KYC sectors, which are always local.
