Executive read

This edition re-underwrites every model against Latka Iran benchmarks, fully loaded 1405 payroll (Tamin, Eidi, Sanavat), channel commissions, SAYAD cheque risk and 7-9% monthly inflation. Average standalone EBITDA margin is 25.6%; shared-engine portfolio EBITDA reaches 46.0B Toman per month.

Seventy-three services sit in Tier P1: 106 are strong standalone wedges, 84 are viable standalone or strong portfolio add-ons, and 10 only work as shared-engine add-ons. Only two services (#104, #140) show negative standalone EBITDA and must be managed through portfolio sales; thin-margin tools follow the same route.

English USD columns use the project rate of 270,000 Toman; the source audit used 203,100. Toman remains the authoritative currency throughout this project.

Methodology

Models use a 12-month ramp with Latka-calibrated customer counts (22-40 enterprise, 115-205 SME, 340-480 prosumer/micro), blended ARPU and 4-11% monthly churn. Costs include domestic GPU with a 25% buffer, NIN, SMS/Shaparak, 12-18% partner commissions, fully loaded tech/sales/support payroll, Tehran office and marketing.
