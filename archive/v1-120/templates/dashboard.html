The thesis in one page

Iran is a large digital market with low consumer ARPU and a small but fast-growing startup base. The winning AI product is not a translated global SaaS. It is a Persian-first workflow wired into local rails: Moadian e-invoicing, Shaparak payments, Shahkar identity, SMS/OTP, Digikala and Basalam seller panels, Divar listings, and Iranian tax and labor rules.

Profit concentrates in boring,高频 back-office and revenue workflows: tax reconciliation, bookkeeping, contract review, clinic reception, real-estate lead qualification, resume screening, review management, seller operations, tender writing, and document AI. These have measurable ROI, recurring usage, and integrations that global tools cannot easily copy.

Avoid generic horizontal chatbots and ad-supported consumer apps. They face global alternatives, high support costs, weak willingness to pay, and constant feature catch-up.

Phase 2 rule: pick two services, run 20 customer interviews, sign 5 paid pilots, and require payback under 6 months before adding a third product line.

How the score works

Every service is scored 1-5 on demand (25%), willingness to pay (20%), feasibility (20%), defensibility (20%), and capital efficiency (15%). The weighted average becomes a 0-100 score.

The score is deliberately biased toward execution fit in Iran, not total addressable market. A large market with a 12-month sales cycle and a foreign API dependency scores lower than a boring workflow with 500 ready buyers and a local integration moat.

Price bands are indicative USD equivalents. In practice, local customers pay in IRR, often annually and in advance, with a contractual repricing clause to manage inflation.

Operating constraints

Payments: local Shaparak rails, weak recurring mandates, wallets and annual prepay.

Compute: constrained GPU access, local-first hosting, small models, caching, on-prem option.

Connectivity: periodic disruption; SMS, offline mode, and local mirrors are required.

Currency: 32-44% inflation; quarterly repricing and USD-linked export tiers.

Trust: pilots, references, Enamad/Samandehi, and private deployment win deals.
